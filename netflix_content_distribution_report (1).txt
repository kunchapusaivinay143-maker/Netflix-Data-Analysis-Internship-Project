NETFLIX CONTENT DISTRIBUTION ANALYSIS

Dataset: netflix_cleaned.csv
Total titles analysed: 8,790

Content breakdown:
- Movies: 6,126 (69.69%)
- TV Shows: 2,664 (30.31%)

Key findings:
1. Movies account for 69.69% of the dataset.
2. TV Shows account for 30.31% of the dataset.
3. There are 3,462 more Movies than TV Shows.
4. There are approximately 2.30 Movies for every TV Show.

Conclusion:
The dataset is movie-dominant: Movies make up the larger share of available titles,
while TV Shows represent a smaller but substantial portion. This comparison describes
the composition of this dataset, not necessarily Netflix's live catalogue.
